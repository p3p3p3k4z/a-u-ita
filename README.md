## 🌳 About the Project

Añi Ita (Soul of the Flower) is a comprehensive solution that transforms how we produce food, demonstrating that it's possible to cultivate crops and, simultaneously, **regenerate life**.

Instead of viewing agriculture and ecology as opposing forces, our project unites them. We create **"Pollinator Islands"** — living ecological engines — that enhance agricultural productivity while actively **restoring biodiversity**.

To make science actionable, the project merges:

* **NASA Earth Observation Data**
* **Artificial Intelligence and Predictive Models**
* **National Data** (INEGI, CONABIO, SAGARPA)
* **Ethnobotanical Knowledge**
* **Immersive 3D Design and Virtual Reality (VR)**

The result is a platform that not only monitors and plans but also **inspires and reconnects** communities with nature, ensuring a sustainable, resilient, and culturally-rooted agricultural future.

## 🛰️ Addressing the NASA "BloomWatch" Challenge

This project directly addresses the **NASA BloomWatch** challenge. We transform NASA's Earth observation data into scalable, real-time insights.

The Phenological Satellite Module uses data from **AVIRIS/EMIT** to detect spectral signatures sensitive to anthocyanins and carotenoids, allowing us to create a **Flowering Index (IFE)**. This index is crucial for:

* Detecting large-scale blooms.
* Identifying **"nectar gaps"** (periods and locations without food for pollinators).
* Guiding rewilding and sustainable agriculture strategies by indicating where and when to plant native flora to create effective **biological corridors**.

## 🧠 Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is a central tool for analyzing, predicting, and visualizing environmental data, as well as for designing educational experiences.

Two main AI models were developed:

1.  **Predictive Life Zones Model:** Uses classification and regression algorithms to identify regions with the highest probability of survival for flora and pollinator species. It considers variables such as climate, altitude, humidity, and soil type.
2.  **National Interactive Mapping Model:** Integrates data from INEGI, SAGARPA, CONABIO, and Google Earth to generate **dynamic maps** with information on biodiversity, crops, climate, and ecological recommendations.

Additionally, AI is used to process satellite images (Landsat, MODIS/VIIRS, AVIRIS/EMIT) and generate the phenological indices that predict flowering.
